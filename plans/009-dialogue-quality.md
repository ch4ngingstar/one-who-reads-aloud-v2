The owner likes the narrator and dislikes the character delivery. The product target is a cast the owner prefers in an actual conversation, with a 10-minute chapter available in at most 40 minutes on their Windows RTX 4070 12 GB. Keep the accepted narrator profile, reference, pronunciation and pacing as a control. Make Fish S2 Pro the first dialogue challenger. Use one GPU in sequence. A separate director assigns speakers and gives acting directions. Continue using Qwen3-14B Q4_K_M until a text-only test earns a replacement.

- `profiles/full-cast.json` routes narration to OmniVoice and all characters to IndexTTS 2.5. In `src/speech_providers.py:30`, Index receives `_synthesize(text, cue.reference, vec, alpha)`. The actor receives no previous reply, addressee or scene objective. An isolated “confused” reading can sound wrong even when the name, words and voice identity are right.
- In `data/benchmarks/ch-156-998055wm/ch_0001.performance.json`, 46 passages are narrator prose, 18 spoken dialogue and 6 Sunny thoughts. The reference clips are Narrator 11.42 s, Sunny 13.23 s, Nephis 12.00 s, Effie 13.50 s. Both the narrator and Effie references are mono 44.1 kHz. Duration and sample rate give no basis for attributing the preference to recording quality. Narration and dialogue differ simultaneously in actor, reference, renderer, direction and material.
- Prior listening notes in `docs/design/voice-foundry.md` favor Cassie and conversational references. Sunny and Nephis were weak under several emotion strengths. Some voices have since been recut. Treat that history as a hypothesis for those actors. Include Cassie as a positive control and Effie as the test for overacting. Existing references, including previously preferred edits, stay available.
- Chapter 1745, “Missing Catalyst” (library DB ID 156), took 421.35 s for 524.63 s of audio. The director took 50.8 s plus 7.8 s to load. One large whole-chapter ASR discrepancy disappeared when the published audio was transcribed at the exact line offsets. `src/take_review.py:8` is a text check. A clean transcript earns no points for acting. At ten minutes, the cold end-to-end budget is 2,400 s, equivalent to 4.0 wall seconds per audio second. Do not extrapolate a narration-heavy chapter to a dialogue-heavy one.

- Compare current Qwen3-14B against the same model with scene-local participant/context evidence, then one local challenger, Qwen3.5-9B text-only GGUF, if supported by the installed llama.cpp build. - Produce character-only exact speaker accuracy, macro accuracy by actor, thought/dialogue confusion, unresolved/fallback rate, invalid outputs, time and VRAM. Report counts and inspect each disagreement. Protect manually corrected labels. Prose volume must not mask poor dialogue accuracy. Proposed swap gate: at least five fewer attribution errors per hundred held-out character turns, no increase in silent guesses, and no repeat of previously fixed critical exchanges. Keep Qwen3-14B if results are tied or inconclusive. If both struggle, surface uncertain lines with evidence for review and try a stronger second pass **only on those scenes**.
- Separately compare **delivery directions** with the selected TTS, fixed text, correct speaker and reference. A compact schema could carry `kind, speaker_id, addressee_id?, intent?, delivery?, evidence_span?, needs_review`. Example: “asks for clarification while trying to sound composed” versus “confused”. Many lines deserve no style instruction. Reject unsupported labels, apply context across adjacent text chunks but reset at scene/POV boundaries, and never let the director rewrite spoken prose. Score direction by holding TTS inputs constant except for the note. Accurate casting alone is insufficient to claim better acting.

## Model and runtime sources

- [Official Fish S2 Pro](https://huggingface.co/fishaudio/s2-pro) and [technical report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.08823) describe reference conditioning, multi-speaker synthesis and natural-language control. The report's serving benchmark uses an H200.
- [Fish installation guide](https://speech.fish.audio/install/) recommends 24 GB VRAM for S2 Pro and at least 48 GB for Drama 3 FP8. [Fish inference guide](https://speech.fish.audio/inference/) documents reference audio and transcript usage. Consult the model's terms before distribution.
- [Community s2.cpp runtime](https://github.com/rodrigomatta/s2.cpp) and [GGUF publisher](https://huggingface.co/subspecs/s2-pro-gguf) are local candidates. Their availability does not establish quality, feature parity, or speed on this PC.
- [Qwen3-14B](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-14B) and [Qwen3.5-9B](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.5-9B) are the proposed text-direction comparison. Model-card scores alone do not measure audiobook attribution.
